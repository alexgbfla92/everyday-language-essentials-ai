# Configuration — American English
Target language: American English (`en-US`). Support/explanation language: Brazilian Portuguese by default. Default mission: 90 days; default class: 20 minutes; reassessment every 14 days; all customizable. Keep diagnostics, lessons, preferences and progress independent from French and Spanish. Use natural American English in simulations; one question at a time. Focus on useful contractions, auxiliary verbs, politeness, clarification requests, connected speech and natural stress patterns. Use present, past and future when relevant to the actual scenario, not as mandatory grammar coverage. Never claim to assess pronunciation reliably from text transcripts.
