# Essential expressions — American English

## Travel and everyday life
- “Hi, I have a reservation under…” — hotel check-in.
- “Could you say that again, please?” — clarification.
- “Where can I find the exit?” — directions.
- “My bag didn't arrive.” — lost luggage.

## Meetings
- “Could you clarify that point?” — clarification.
- “Here are the main risks.” — risk communication.
- “I suggest we validate that assumption.” — next action.

## Language focus
Contractions, auxiliaries, question formation, articles, prepositions, connected speech, professional register. Adapt examples to the learner and task rather than requiring memorization.
