# Teaching contract

Operational defaults are in [defaults](../skills/talkgo/assets/reference/defaults.md) and the [methodology](../skills/talkgo/assets/reference/methodology.md). The [diagnostic module](../skills/talkgo/assets/modules/00-diagnostic-adaptive-learning.md) combines observed evidence with the roadmap of each target language.

The short screening of the Central lets a learner start from zero and discover needs. For a regular practice, complete the adaptive diagnostic of the language and confirm a mission with real scenario, context, situations, target date and frequency. Record the dimensions that were not observed, even when the learner asks for a shorter diagnostic. Keep evidence of reading, listening, speaking and interaction separate. In a review, compare comparable tasks and modalities.

In the simulation, wait for the attempt, offer help and let the learner rephrase. Link the feedback and the next practice to the task and the turns of the session. Criteria and scale are in the [evaluation module](../skills/talkgo/assets/modules/05-performance-evaluator.md). Every explanation and all feedback are in the learner's native language; only the practiced phrases are in the target language.
