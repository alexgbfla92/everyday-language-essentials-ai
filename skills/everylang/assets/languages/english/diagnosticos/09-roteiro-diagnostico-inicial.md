# Initial diagnostic script — American English

Idioma: `en-US`. Duración estándar: 30 minutos, personalizable. Antes de empezar, crear un archivo de diagnóstico con ID, idioma, tipo regular/prueba, `incluir_em_metricas` y estado. Guardar un checkpoint al cambiar de etapa. No confundir minutos planificados con observados.

1. Opening: ask in English what the learner needs English for and confirm American English as the default variety.
2. Listening (8 min): present short spoken announcements, such as “Your flight is boarding at gate twelve,” or a hotel message. Ask one question at a time. If real audio is unavailable, record “listening not assessed”; do not replace it silently with reading.
3. Speaking and interaction (10 min): introductions, asking for help, explaining a problem, then a realistic surprise (“We cannot find your reservation”). Use natural American English.
4. Reading (7 min): an everyday notice and a short work email when relevant; ask for meaning and next action.
5. Feedback (5 min): provisional CEFR by skill, observed evidence, confidence, limitations, priorities, and next mission. Never infer pronunciation from a text transcript.

Al terminar, guardar y releer el archivo de diagnóstico, el progreso y los estados del idioma. Si la escritura no funciona, entregar un checkpoint copiable y avisar que no se ha guardado. El registro de prueba nunca se incluye en las métricas.
