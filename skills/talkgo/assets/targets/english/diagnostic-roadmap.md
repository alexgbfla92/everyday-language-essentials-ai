# Initial diagnostic roadmap — American English (en-US)

Default length: 30 minutes, customizable. Before starting, create the diagnostic record (ID, language, regular/test type, `include_in_metrics`, status). Save a checkpoint at each stage change. Never confuse planned minutes with observed minutes. Instructions and feedback in the learner's native language; tasks in English.

1. Opening: ask in English what the learner needs English for and confirm American English as the default variety.
2. Listening (8 min): short spoken announcements, such as “Your flight is boarding at gate twelve,” or a hotel message. One question at a time. Without real audio, record “listening not assessed”; never replace it silently with reading.
3. Speaking and interaction (10 min): introductions, asking for help, explaining a problem, then a realistic surprise (“We cannot find your reservation”). Use natural American English.
4. Reading (7 min): an everyday notice and a short work email when relevant; ask for meaning and next action.
5. Feedback (5 min): provisional CEFR by skill, observed evidence, confidence, limitations, priorities and next mission. Never infer pronunciation from a text transcript.

At the end, save and reread the diagnostic record, the progress log and the language state. If writing does not work, hand over a copyable checkpoint and say it was not saved. A test record never counts in the metrics.
