# Notes — French (France) (fr-FR)

Explanations in the learner's native language. Keep diagnostics, practices, preferences and progress independent. Simulation prompts, dialogues and language feedback use natural French, never literal translations from English. Prefer «vous» in professional contexts and with strangers; explain «tu» by context. Work on liaison, nasal vowels, the /y/ and /u/ sounds, elision and intonation, without claiming to judge pronunciation from a transcript. Introduce the grammar structures useful for the task, not a fixed list of English tenses.
