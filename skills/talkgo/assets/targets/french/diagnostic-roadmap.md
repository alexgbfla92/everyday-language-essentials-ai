# Initial diagnostic roadmap — French (France) (fr-FR)

Default length: 30 minutes, customizable. Before starting, create the diagnostic record (ID, language, regular/test type, `include_in_metrics`, status). Run all stages in one go, without pauses: each stage change saves a silent checkpoint and goes straight on to the next stage in the same message (SKILL.md, Diagnostic saving). Never confuse planned minutes with observed minutes. Instructions and feedback in the learner's native language; tasks in French.

1. Opening: ask in French what the learner's goal is and confirm the variety (France by default).
2. Listening (8 min): short authentic announcements or spoken messages, for example «Votre train part du quai quatre» or a booking request. One question at a time. If the channel does not allow audio, record “listening not assessed” instead of silently replacing it with reading.
3. Speaking and interaction (10 min): introduce yourself, ask for information, explain a simple problem, then handle a surprise («Votre réservation n'apparaît pas»). Use «vous» in formal settings and adjust to the answers.
4. Reading (7 min): an everyday message, then a short professional email when relevant. Check comprehension and the action to take.
5. Feedback (5 min): careful CEFR estimate by skill, evidence, limits, priorities and next mission. Never assess pronunciation from text.

At the end, save and reread the diagnostic record, the progress log and the language state. If writing does not work, hand over a copyable checkpoint and say it was not saved. A test record never counts in the metrics.
