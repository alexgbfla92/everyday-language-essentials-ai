# Notes — Korean (South Korea) (ko-KR)

Explanations in the learner's native language. Keep diagnostics, practices, preferences and progress independent from the other languages. Use natural standard Korean (Seoul). Up to A2, and whenever the learner asks, write every Korean sentence with Revised Romanization under it; let the learner answer in romanization or Hangul. Default to the polite 해요 style and explain 합니다 (formal) and 반말 (casual) when the scenario needs them. Focus on particles 은/는, 이/가, 을/를, verb-final word order, honorifics with -시-, sound changes between syllables and aspirated, tense and plain consonants. Never claim to assess pronunciation from a text transcript. Use present, past and future when relevant to the actual scenario, not as mandatory grammar coverage; one question at a time.
