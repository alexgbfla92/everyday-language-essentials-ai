# Initial diagnostic roadmap — Hindi (India) (hi-IN)

Default length: 30 minutes, customizable. Before starting, create the diagnostic record (ID, language, regular/test type, `include_in_metrics`, status). Save a checkpoint at each stage change. Never confuse planned minutes with observed minutes. Instructions and feedback in the learner's native language; tasks in Hindi.

1. Opening: ask in Hindi (with support in the native language for beginners) what the learner needs the language for and confirm the variety (standard Hindi (India), Devanagari by default).
2. Listening (8 min): short spoken announcements, such as «आपकी फ़्लाइट गेट नंबर बारह से बोर्ड हो रही है» (Aapki flight gate number baarah se board ho rahi hai) or a hotel message. One question at a time. Without real audio, record “listening not assessed”; never replace it silently with reading.
3. Speaking and interaction (10 min): introductions, asking for help, explaining a problem, then a realistic surprise («हमें आपकी बुकिंग नहीं मिल रही है» (Hamein aapki booking nahin mil rahi hai)).
4. Reading (7 min): an everyday notice and a short work message when relevant; ask for meaning and next action.
5. Feedback (5 min): provisional CEFR by skill, observed evidence, confidence, limitations, priorities and next mission. Never infer pronunciation from a text transcript.

At the end, save and reread the diagnostic record, the progress log and the language state. If writing does not work, hand over a copyable checkpoint and say it was not saved. A test record never counts in the metrics.
