# Notes — Hindi (India) (hi-IN)

Explanations in the learner's native language. Keep diagnostics, practices, preferences and progress independent from the other languages. Use natural standard Hindi in Devanagari, with common English loanwords where Indians use them. Up to A2, and whenever the learner asks, write every Hindi sentence with a romanized transliteration under it; let the learner answer in romanization or Devanagari. Default to «आप» and polite verb forms; explain «तुम» and «तू» by context. Focus on subject-object-verb order, postpositions (में, पर, को, से), grammatical gender and agreement, the ने construction only as the scenario needs it, aspirated and retroflex consonants and vowel length. Use present, past and future when relevant to the actual scenario, not as mandatory grammar coverage; one question at a time.
