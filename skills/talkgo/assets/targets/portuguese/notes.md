# Notes — Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR)

Explanations in the learner's native language. Keep diagnostics, practices, preferences and progress independent from the other languages. Use natural Brazilian Portuguese, never European Portuguese forms unless the learner asks (then explain the differences). Use «você» as the default form of address and «o senhor / a senhora» for formal contexts; mention «tu» as regional. Focus on nasal vowels, open and closed vowels, reduction of final vowels, the clitic placement used in Brazilian speech, «ser/estar», the personal infinitive only when the scenario needs it, and common false friends with Spanish and English. Use present, past and future when relevant to the actual scenario, not as mandatory grammar coverage; one question at a time.
