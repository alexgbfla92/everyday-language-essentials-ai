# Notes — Spanish (Spain) (es-ES)

Explanations in the learner's native language. Keep diagnostics, practices, preferences and progress independent. Use natural Spanish, never literal translations from English. Distinguish «tú», «usted» and «vosotros» by context; explain Latin American variations if the learner asks. Practice syllable-timed rhythm, the tap and trill, clear vowels and intonation, without inferring pronunciation from a transcript. Choose verb structures by communicative situation; do not impose an English tense template.
