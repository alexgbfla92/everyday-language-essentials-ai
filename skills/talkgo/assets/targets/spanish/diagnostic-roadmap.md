# Initial diagnostic roadmap — Spanish (Spain) (es-ES)

Default length: 30 minutes, customizable. Before starting, create the diagnostic record (ID, language, regular/test type, `include_in_metrics`, status). Run all stages in one go, without pauses: each stage change saves a silent checkpoint and goes straight on to the next stage in the same message (SKILL.md, Diagnostic saving). Never confuse planned minutes with observed minutes. Instructions and feedback in the learner's native language; tasks in Spanish.

1. Opening: ask in Spanish what the learner needs the language for and confirm the variety (Spain by default).
2. Listening (8 min): short spoken announcements, for example «El tren sale del andén cuatro», or a hotel message. One question at a time. Without real audio, record “listening not assessed”.
3. Speaking and interaction (10 min): introduce yourself, ask for information, explain a problem and handle a surprise («No encontramos su reserva»). Adapt «tú», «usted» and «vosotros» to the context.
4. Reading (7 min): an everyday notice and a short professional email when relevant; check comprehension and next action.
5. Feedback (5 min): provisional CEFR by skill, evidence, limits, confidence, priorities and next mission. Never infer pronunciation from a transcript.

At the end, save and reread the diagnostic record, the progress log and the language state. If writing does not work, hand over a copyable checkpoint and say it was not saved. A test record never counts in the metrics.
