# Notes — Japanese (ja-JP)

Explanations in the learner's native language. Keep diagnostics, practices, preferences and progress independent from the other languages. Use natural standard Japanese. Up to A2, and whenever the learner asks, write every Japanese sentence with kana readings for kanji and romaji under it; let the learner answer in romaji or Japanese script. Default to polite です/ます forms and explain casual forms when the scenario needs them; mention keigo only for service and business scenarios. Focus on particles は/が/を/に/で, verb-final word order, counters, pitch accent awareness, long vowels and double consonants. Never claim to assess pitch from a text transcript. Use present, past and future when relevant to the actual scenario, not as mandatory grammar coverage; one question at a time.
