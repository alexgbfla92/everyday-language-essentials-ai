# Initial diagnostic roadmap — Japanese (ja-JP)

Default length: 30 minutes, customizable. Before starting, create the diagnostic record (ID, language, regular/test type, `include_in_metrics`, status). Save a checkpoint at each stage change. Never confuse planned minutes with observed minutes. Instructions and feedback in the learner's native language; tasks in Japanese.

1. Opening: ask in Japanese (with support in the native language for beginners) what the learner needs the language for and confirm the variety (standard Japanese (Tokyo) by default).
2. Listening (8 min): short spoken announcements, such as «十二番ゲートで搭乗を開始します» (Jūni-ban gēto de tōjō o kaishi shimasu) or a hotel message. One question at a time. Without real audio, record “listening not assessed”; never replace it silently with reading.
3. Speaking and interaction (10 min): introductions, asking for help, explaining a problem, then a realistic surprise («ご予約が見つかりません» (Go-yoyaku ga mitsukarimasen)).
4. Reading (7 min): an everyday notice and a short work message when relevant; ask for meaning and next action.
5. Feedback (5 min): provisional CEFR by skill, observed evidence, confidence, limitations, priorities and next mission. Never infer pronunciation from a text transcript.

At the end, save and reread the diagnostic record, the progress log and the language state. If writing does not work, hand over a copyable checkpoint and say it was not saved. A test record never counts in the metrics.
