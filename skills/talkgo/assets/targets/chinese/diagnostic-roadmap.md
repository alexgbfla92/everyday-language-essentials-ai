# Initial diagnostic roadmap — Mandarin Chinese (Simplified) (zh-CN)

Default length: 30 minutes, customizable. Before starting, create the diagnostic record (ID, language, regular/test type, `include_in_metrics`, status). Run all stages in one go, without pauses: each stage change saves a silent checkpoint and goes straight on to the next stage in the same message (SKILL.md, Diagnostic saving). Never confuse planned minutes with observed minutes. Instructions and feedback in the learner's native language; tasks in Mandarin Chinese.

1. Opening: ask in Mandarin Chinese (with support in the native language for beginners) what the learner needs the language for and confirm the variety (Mainland China, simplified characters and pinyin by default).
2. Listening (8 min): short spoken announcements, such as «您的航班在十二号登机口登机» (Nín de hángbān zài shí'èr hào dēngjīkǒu dēngjī) or a hotel message. One question at a time. Without real audio, record “listening not assessed”; never replace it silently with reading.
3. Speaking and interaction (10 min): introductions, asking for help, explaining a problem, then a realistic surprise («我们找不到您的预订» (Wǒmen zhǎo bú dào nín de yùdìng)).
4. Reading (7 min): an everyday notice and a short work message when relevant; ask for meaning and next action.
5. Feedback (5 min): provisional CEFR by skill, observed evidence, confidence, limitations, priorities and next mission. Never infer pronunciation from a text transcript.

At the end, save and reread the diagnostic record, the progress log and the language state. If writing does not work, hand over a copyable checkpoint and say it was not saved. A test record never counts in the metrics.
