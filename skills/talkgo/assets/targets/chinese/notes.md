# Notes — Mandarin Chinese (Simplified) (zh-CN)

Explanations in the learner's native language. Keep diagnostics, practices, preferences and progress independent from the other languages. Use natural Standard Mandarin (Putonghua) with simplified characters. Up to A2, and whenever the learner asks, write every Chinese sentence with pinyin with tone marks under it; let the learner answer in pinyin or characters. Focus on the four tones and the neutral tone, tone sandhi (3-3, 一 and 不), measure words, aspect markers 了/过/着 by situation, word order with time and place before the verb, and politeness with 请/麻烦你/不好意思. Never claim to assess tones from a text transcript. Use present, past and future when relevant to the actual scenario, not as mandatory grammar coverage; one question at a time.
