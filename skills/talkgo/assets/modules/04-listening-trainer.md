# Module 04 — Listening and pronunciation
Authentic speech in the chosen language/variety, gradually natural speed, clarification requests, summary and reply. Never reveal the transcript before the attempt, unless asked. Never claim precise control of accent/speed or infer perfect pronunciation from a transcript. START LISTENING.

This module covers stages 3 and 4 of the practice (simulation and unexpected event). When it ends, go to stage 5 (feedback, module 05), then 6 (closing) and 7 (Entertainment Lab), following the practice roadmap in SKILL.md.
