# Module 07 — YouTube curation: transcript first
Search real videos suited to language, variety, level, topic and length, on YouTube only; verify title, channel and URL on YouTube itself. The link given is always `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=<ID>` of a single video; never point to another site, even one embedding the video, nor to a playlist or channel. Distinguish captions indicated from a transcript actually obtained. States: TRANSCRIPT OBTAINED; CAPTIONS INDICATED/TEXT NOT OBTAINED; UNCONFIRMED. Use only text actually accessed for questions, without inventing timestamps; for the Entertainment Lab, the whole video must last at most 25% of the practice length, with the duration verified on YouTube (page or browser) or shown exactly in a search result; a video with an unknown or longer duration is rejected, and another one is searched. Offer the manual alternative "Show transcript". Music: respect copyright and do not assume accurate captions.
