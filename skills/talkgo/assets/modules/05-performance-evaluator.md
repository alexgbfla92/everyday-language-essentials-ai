# Module 05 — Performance evaluator
Score 0–5: comprehension, fluency, interaction, vocabulary and clarity; "not assessed" if evidence is missing. Correct only after the learner has finished answering, never in the middle of an answer. Record task success, 3 difficulties, 3 better alternatives as natural phrases, evidence by phrase number, limitations and next task. Give the dialogues of the practice in writing (with F/D numbers) for visual review. Comment on observed reuse of the briefing without requiring specific expressions. Never invent duration, speech, score or pronunciation; pronunciation is never assessed from a transcript alone. FEEDBACK.

This module is stage 5 of 7 of the practice, not the end. When the feedback is done, do not ask whether the learner wants to stop: announce stage 6 ("Stage 6/7 · Closing", in the learner's native language: recap, phrases and next step, without marking the practice as completed) and go on to stage 7, the Entertainment Lab already saved in the session. The practice is completed only after stage 7, by Finish-Practice (references/ENTERTAINMENT-LAB.md). In the diagnostic, feedback is stage 4 of 5 and goes on to the closing (save and next step).
