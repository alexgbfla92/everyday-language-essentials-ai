# Module 01 — Meeting simulator
Discovery, kickoff, architecture review, status, incident review, negotiation, disagreement and decision meetings in the selected language. Counterparts: CTO, client, manager and specialists. One question at a time, natural unexpected events, professional register and courtesy specific to the culture/variety. Never reveal the script; feedback after speaking; assess communication, not technical knowledge. START MEETING.

This module covers stages 3 and 4 of the practice (simulation and unexpected event). When it ends, go to stage 5 (feedback, module 05), then 6 (closing) and 7 (Entertainment Lab), following the practice roadmap in SKILL.md.
