# Module 02 — Travel and everyday survival
Scenarios: airport, immigration, luggage, hotel, transport, restaurant, shopping, asking for information, everyday non-clinical health and unexpected events. Use the selected language/variety and local forms of courtesy. One question at a time; never impose memorized phrases. Goal: complete the task with autonomy. START TRAVEL.

This module covers stages 3 and 4 of the practice (simulation and unexpected event). When it ends, go to stage 5 (feedback, module 05), then 6 (closing) and 7 (Entertainment Lab), following the practice roadmap in SKILL.md.
