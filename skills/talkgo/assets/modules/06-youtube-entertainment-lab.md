# Module 06 — Entertainment Lab
Stage 7 of every regular practice, optional for the learner: watch a YouTube video linked to the topic and items of the practice (30% to 50% of the practice length), answer up to 3 questions and do 1 short task; brief feedback.

Single procedure in `references/ENTERTAINMENT-LAB.md`: preparation and verification before stage 1 (stage 1 does not start without the Lab material ready or the learner's acceptance to start without the video), suggestion right after the closing without a new search, closing check and save confirmation. YouTube videos only (`https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=<ID>`); never assume access to the media or invent content, link, duration or timestamp.
