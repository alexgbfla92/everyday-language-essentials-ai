# Module 06 — Entertainment Lab
Stage 7 of every regular practice, part of the practice and of its length, executed before the completion: watch a YouTube video linked to the topic and items of the practice, of at most 25% of the planned length (`max_video_seconds = practice_minutes × 60 × 0.25`: 7 min 30 s in a 30-minute practice), answer the comprehension questions (1 to 3) and do 1 short task; brief feedback.

Single procedure in `references/ENTERTAINMENT-LAB.md`: preparation and verification of the real duration before stage 1, execution as stage 7 right after stage 6, and only then Finish-Practice. A video with an unknown duration or longer than the limit is never official material. YouTube videos only (`https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=<ID>`); never assume access to the media or invent content, link, duration or timestamp.
